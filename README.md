## What it does

`main.py` writes one line of weather forecast — 140 characters at most — for a city that does not exist, to `/output/entry.txt`. The forecast names the fictional city of Snozzleville, gives it light drizzle, and turns a late bus into a small win for a bored commuter.

## Entry

> Good morning, Snozzleville: light drizzle, 14°C, and the 8:04 bus is running 3 minutes late — just long enough to finish your coffee.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Deterministic: no network, no randomness, no input data.
